=====
Pages
=====

The dashboard shows your projects as cards with a few numbers and a menu; the tab **Add repositories** lists your repositories, those of your organisations and those you contributed to, and adds any other as ``owner/name`` or ``owner``.

Every statistics page has a bar that shows how complete its data is, **Data and sources** with the repositories, the downloads of the data and the choice of sources, and a time range.

**Pull requests and issues** lists them with their first response, filtered by kind, state, author and whether somebody responded. **Compare repositories** shows one row per repository, for an organisation.

**Downloads** shows the fetches that run, those that wait, why they were started, and what ran before.

**GitHub access** holds the linked GitHub account, your tokens, their order and limits, and the settings of fetches. **Profile** shows your email addresses, password, linked GitHub account and tokens, with the pages to change them. **Messages** lists what the site told you, such as that a file is ready. **Classes** holds your classes of people.

The **About** page shows the version, the commit and the licenses of all software installed.
