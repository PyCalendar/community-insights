=====================
Manage your account
=====================

The page **Profile** in the menu shows your email addresses, your password, the linked GitHub account and your tokens. The changes are made on the pages it links to.

Confirm an email address
========================

An address marked *not confirmed* has not been confirmed yet.

#. Press **Send the confirmation again** next to it.
#. Open the link in the email the site sends you.

To add or remove an address, or to choose the one you sign in with, press **Add, remove or choose the primary address**.

Change or reset the password
============================

- Press **Change the password**, enter the current one and the new one twice.
- If you signed up with GitHub and have no password yet, press **Set a password**.
- If you forgot the password, press **Reset the password by email**, or use *Forgot your password?* on the sign in page. The email has a link to choose a new one.

Link or unlink GitHub
=====================

- Without a linked account, press **Link GitHub account** and allow the site on GitHub. The site then reads GitHub with your account first.
- To unlink it, press **Manage linked accounts** and remove it there. Keep a password or a token, so you can still sign in and read GitHub.

An installation without the OAuth app of GitHub cannot link accounts; add tokens instead, see :doc:`github-oauth`.

Manage tokens
=============

The profile lists your tokens and marks those that expired or expire within two weeks. Press **Add, order, test and share tokens** to change them on **GitHub access**, see :doc:`tokens`.
