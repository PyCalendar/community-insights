=====
Pages
=====

The dashboard shows your projects as cards with a few numbers, the button **Update** and a menu; the tab **Add repositories** lists your repositories, those of your organisations and those you contributed to, and adds any other as ``owner/name`` or ``owner``.

The pages of a project are in one drop-down at the top of each of them: **Overview**, **Response times**, **Contributors**, **Pull requests and issues**, **Replies**, **Badges**, and **Compare repositories** for several repositories; the cards of the dashboard and of **Explore** offer the same pages (:doc:`../how-to/use`). The pages of a person have their own drop-down in the path at the top.

Every statistics page has a bar that shows how complete its data is, with **Update**, which reads all the pages need, and its drop-down to the page that estimates and chooses what to read; **Data and sources** with the repositories, the downloads of the data and the choice of sources; and a time range. On a desktop, the heading of a table stays at the top of the window while the page scrolls.

**Pull requests and issues** lists them with their first response, filtered by kind, state, author and whether somebody responded. **Compare repositories** shows one row per repository, for an organisation. **Replies** shows whether the authors of pull requests replied to the comments and reviews they got; the label of a person opens their replies, with all their pull requests and their badge, whose page has its code; see :doc:`replies` and :ref:`reply-badges`. The numbers of pull requests and issues at the top of the page of a person open their list of pull requests, issues or both.

**Downloads** shows the fetches that run, those that wait, why they were started, and what ran before.

**GitHub access** holds the linked GitHub account, your tokens, their order and limits, and the settings of fetches. **Profile** shows your email addresses, password, linked GitHub account and tokens, with the pages to change them. **Messages** lists what the site told you, such as that a file is ready. **Classes** holds your classes of people.

The **About** page shows the version, the commit and the licenses of all software installed. The footer of every page shows the version, the choice of the language and the time zone.
