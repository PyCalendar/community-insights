==================
Your first project
==================

In this tutorial you add a project, read it from GitHub and look at its statistics: how fast issues and pull requests get a first response, and who contributes.

You need an installation with an account that can read GitHub: :doc:`set-up`. On https://insight.pycal.org you can follow it, too, with public repositories. It takes about 10 minutes, depending on the size of the project.

Add the project and read it
===========================

#. On the dashboard choose the tab **Add repositories** and press **Refresh from GitHub**: your repositories, those of your organisations and those you contributed to are listed.
#. Type a part of a name to filter them, or type any ``owner/name`` or an organisation, and choose it.
#. Open the project from its card. The bar at the top says that 0% of the data is there.
#. Press **Update**. The site starts to read at once; the drop-down next to it opens the page that estimates the API points and the time and chooses what and how much to read. The bar fills while the data comes in, and **Downloads** shows the fetch.

Look at the statistics
======================

#. When the bar reaches 100%, the overview shows the response times: how long pull requests and issues waited for a first response, for each class of people.
#. Open **Contributors**: everybody who contributed, with their pull requests, issues, comments and reviews. Click a column title to sort by it.
#. Open a person: their contributions per month, and when in the day and the week they are active.
#. At the bottom of the overview, build a badge of the response time for your README.

You have a project with its statistics. Go on with :doc:`replies`.
